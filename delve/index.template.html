YOU ONLY KEEP WHAT YOU CARRY OUT

Relics are down there. Die and you lose every one of them. Reach a way out and they are yours.

Tap a tile beside you to step. Step into something to strike it.

Everything down here tells you what it is about to do before you move — and then does it, even if you have gone. Red tiles are struck this turn. Amber tiles are struck next.

Two stairs, and each one tells you what is waiting under it — but never how dangerous it is. One of them is always the richer door. That is the whole gamble.

The way out does not always exist — it appears every other floor, so going down is a commitment to two.

Everyone gets the same dungeon today.
