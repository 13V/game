BUILD YOUR KINGDOM

Supplies build buildings. Wood and stone pay for everything. Sawmills make wood, quarries make stone. Pick a building on the left, click the land; one rises each day. Time starts with your first building.

Farms feed the folk. Each farm feeds 4 a day. Not enough farms and they starve.

Houses grow the town. A house adds 4 beds, and fed folk move in on their own. More folk staff more buildings — and pay more tax.

Tax makes gold. Every 10th day each folk pays your tax rate; markets add gold every day. Swap gold for ⟡ groats in the Empire, top right — they carry across valleys and buy permanent head starts.

Follow the goals on the left and you can't go far wrong.
