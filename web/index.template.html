What the wallet does, and does not do. Connecting reads your public key from the Phantom extension, and signing produces a real ed25519 signature over your vault — both happen entirely inside your browser, with no network call. This page can reach no Solana RPC node, so it cannot read your balance and cannot send a transaction, and it will not pretend to. The signed claim is exactly the payload the Anchor program verifies when the groat token ships; until then your groats live in this browser and the signature says whose they are.

KINGDOM

Grow four villagers on a floating island into a kingdom. Keep them fed, give them beds, and take a fair tax — that is the whole game. Every goal you hit and every rank you climb pays ⟡ groats, and groats are yours forever.
1
Supplies build. Wood and stone pay for every building. A sawmill beside the forest cuts 2 wood a day; a quarry beside the rock cuts 2 stone.
2
Farms feed. Every villager eats 1 food a day. One farm grows 4. Watch the green number by FOOD at the top — if it turns red, they are starving.
3
Houses grow you. Each house is 4 beds, and well-fed folk move in on their own. Every villager works one building — so more folk means more of everything.
4
Winter is the test. A year is four seasons, and through the last one farms grow half as much. Thirty lean days. Build a surplus in the green months or the pantry runs dry in the white ones — and if you ever run out of wood, the merchant in the right rail sells supplies for gold.
5
Then it gets deeper. A mine on a seam of gold-flecked ore pays 4 a day. A chapel lifts the mood every few days, and a festival buys goodwill outright — which is what makes a harsh tax a choice rather than a mistake.
6
Everything pays. Each quest in the left rail pays ⟡ groats, and your settlement climbs from Camp to Hamlet, Village, Town, City and finally Kingdom — each rank a bigger purse. Spend groats in the Empire on charters: permanent head starts for every valley you ever settle.
Lost? Quick start in the top bar founds a working hamlet for you.
