HOW TO REIGN

Feed them first. Six villagers, fifteen food — two and a half days. A field takes four hands and feeds eight; place it before anything else, or the hamlet starves by day 8.

The list is the law. Buildings rise one per day, top to bottom, and villagers staff the list in the same order. An unaffordable entry blocks everything behind it.

Coin and the tithe. Wages cost coin; taxes raise it every tenth day. Rate 2 is forgiven while nobody starves. Rate 3 breeds revolt — festivals (20 coin) buy patience back.

The score. Sawmill and mine feed the smithy; the smithy makes goods; roads carry them to market. Production falls with road distance — a far mine loses nearly half.

240 days. Then the ledger closes. Refine the plan and run it again — same valley, same rules, no dice.
